# Scoops adaptive learning v32

Date: 2026-08-08

## v32 corrections

- Spelling Bee attempt status now counts completed tries rather than labeling the next try as though it has already happened.
- Initial status is `0 of 3 tries used · 3 tries left`. After one miss it becomes `1 of 3 tries used · 2 tries left`; after two misses it becomes `2 of 3 tries used · 1 try left`.
- The Spelling Bee attempt status above the writing line and Correct!/Incorrect! feedback below it are centered over the writing line itself. The Typing mode label remains in its existing position.
- Reading Detective CASE EVIDENCE is shifted left so its visual center aligns with the grade-level passage pill rather than sitting to the right of it.
- All v31 behavior is retained, including the corrected bee stinger, three-attempt spelling flow, adaptive diagnostic follow-ups, interest-aware Reading Detective passages, modern navigation, and richer Learn cover art.
